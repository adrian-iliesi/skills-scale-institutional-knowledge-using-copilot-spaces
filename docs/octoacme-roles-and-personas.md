# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Stakeholder Manager

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Managers ensure the right people are informed, aligned, and engaged at the right times throughout the project lifecycle. They translate stakeholder needs into actionable project requirements and help maintain trust and visibility.

### Responsibilities
- Identify and map key stakeholders, sponsors, and decision-makers
- Facilitate requirement gathering and alignment conversations
- Communicate status, risks, and decisions to sponsors and leadership
- Coordinate expectation management and change communication
- Support escalation paths when trade-offs or conflict arise

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Product Managers to clarify business needs and success metrics
- Partners with Project Managers to ensure stakeholder communication plans are integrated into delivery timelines
- Provides context and updates to Developers and leadership when changes affect scope or priorities

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects guide the technical direction of the solution, balancing business needs with long-term design quality, scalability, and maintainability. They help ensure implementation decisions support the overall platform strategy.

### Responsibilities
- Define the technical approach and architecture for the solution
- Review design decisions for maintainability, performance, and scalability
- Identify technical dependencies, constraints, and risks early
- Support engineering teams with standards, patterns, and design guidance
- Align technical decisions with product and delivery goals

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Collaborates with Product Managers to translate business needs into technical options and trade-offs
- Works with Project Managers to assess feasibility, sequence, and delivery risk
- Advises Developers during implementation and reviews to keep the solution aligned with the architecture

---

## Quality Assurance Lead

### Role Summary
Quality Assurance Leads maintain quality standards across the project lifecycle by defining validation approaches, managing test coverage, and ensuring issues are resolved before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define quality gates, acceptance criteria checks, and test strategies
- Coordinate QA planning across build, integration, and release stages
- Track defects, quality trends, and readiness for deployment
- Partner with engineering and product teams to validate changes against expectations
- Recommend remediation paths for quality or compliance gaps

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Works with Developers to validate implementations and identify defects early
- Partners with Product Managers to ensure outcomes meet customer and business expectations
- Coordinates with Project Managers on release readiness and quality-related risk communication

---

## Resource Manager / Scrum Master

### Role Summary
Resource Managers and Scrum Masters help the team operate effectively by managing workload, removing blockers, and supporting healthy delivery practices.

### Responsibilities
- Align work allocation with team capacity and project priorities
- Facilitate Agile ceremonies such as standups, sprint planning, and retrospectives
- Remove blockers that slow delivery or create unnecessary friction
- Support team health, collaboration, and continuous improvement
- Help maintain realistic commitments and transparent progress tracking

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to maintain delivery flow and delivery timing
- Supports Developers by improving team efficiency and reducing execution friction
- Works with Product Managers to clarify backlog priorities and sequencing trade-offs

---

## Change Manager

### Role Summary
Change Managers help teams and stakeholders adopt new ways of working, process changes, or project outcomes with minimal disruption and strong support.

### Responsibilities
- Assess organizational impact of project changes or new workflows
- Define communication and adoption plans for stakeholders and users
- Coordinate training, readiness checks, and transition support
- Identify change risks and mitigation strategies
- Measure adoption and user readiness after rollout

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Aligns with Project Managers on rollout timing, stakeholder communication, and dependencies
- Works with Product Managers to ensure proposed changes support user experience and business outcomes
- Coordinates with Developers and Technical Architects to ensure operational readiness and supportability

---

## Finance / Budget Owner

### Role Summary
Finance or Budget Owners oversee project cost tracking, approvals, and financial health so delivery remains realistic and accountable to the organization.

### Responsibilities
- Track project spend, allocations, and cost trends
- Review budget risks and forecast variances against plan
- Approve or support funding decisions for scope, resources, or contingency needs
- Report financial impact to leadership and project stakeholders
- Support trade-off discussions when scope, timeline, or capacity must change

### Interactions with Existing Roles
- Partners with Project Managers to assess project health against budget and forecast
- Works with Product Managers to align scope priorities with funding realities
- Helps Technical Architects and Developers understand cost trade-offs when evaluating design choices

---
